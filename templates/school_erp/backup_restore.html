Zero-Risk Sandbox Restore Testing (OWASP & GIGW Compliance)

As required by the Government of India website security guidance and OWASP disaster recovery standards, backup archives can be tested for full integrity and data extractability in an isolated sandbox or a dedicated staging environment. Live production tables, academic marks, and fee transactions are 100% isolated and never overwritten during test restore routines.

Mode A: Isolated Sandbox Verification

Unpacks archive in a transient sandbox directory. Validates SHA-256 hash, verifies SQLite database table structures, verifies record counts (students, receipts), and cleans up sandbox automatically.

Ideal for routine compliance audits & integrity certifications.

Mode B: Staging / Test Environment Restore

Extracts database and uploaded document media into a dedicated staging_test/ directory. Developers and auditors can inspect restored SQLite files and media without touching production.

Satisfies Government of India requirement for separate staging recovery.
